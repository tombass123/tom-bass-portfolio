What is Selectors Support?

In 2020, two best friends Tom Bass and Dom Thompson, started a Club Night with the aim to raise money for Charity, whilst showcasing DJs and Artists.

They wanted to create welcoming, expressive spaces where people could dance, smile and contribute to positive social change.

Since 2020 Selectors Support has thrown more than 10 parties, welcomed 1,000 + people, and raised over £9,000 for local and International charities.

Their events have hosted the likes of Haseeb Iqbal, Donna Leake, Kate Ireland, Chunky and Charlie Bones.
